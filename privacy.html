Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Thank you for using the Amazon Insight Extractor ("the Extension"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Extension operates entirely within your local browser environment. We do not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit your personal data.
  • Track your browsing history across the web.
  • Send your extracted Amazon data to any external servers or third-party databases.
  • Collect analytics or telemetry data.

2. Data We Process Locally

In order for the Extension to function correctly, it processes data locally on your machine:

  • Amazon ASIN Data: The extension reads and extracts product information directly from the Amazon pages you authorize it to scan.
  • Local Storage: Scraped data, user settings (such as local postcodes), and progress states are saved directly to your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export it as a CSV or Excel file.

3. Permissions Required

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions, which are strictly necessary for its core functionality:

  • tabs: Required to open new Amazon product pages, inject the scraping scripts, and close the tabs once data extraction is complete.
  • storage: Required to save your extraction results and application settings locally.
  • downloads: Required to allow you to export your compiled data as CSV, Excel, or ZIP files directly to your hard drive.
  • host permissions (*://*.amazon.*/*): Required to interact with Amazon's marketplace domains to read and extract product details.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any third-party advertising or analytics services. The extension relies solely on open-source libraries (e.g., SheetJS for Excel generation) which operate 100% locally on your machine.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new features or comply with Chrome Web Store regulations. All changes will be reflected on this page.
